Background jobs

Slow or failure-prone work runs here instead of making you wait: extracting commitments after an upload or a webhook, retrying a Google Sheets sync that failed, and the automatic reminder check. A job that fails for a temporary reason is retried automatically with a growing delay (30 s, 1 min, 2 min…); one that can't succeed (e.g. the model isn't trained) fails straight away with the reason. Jobs survive an app restart.
